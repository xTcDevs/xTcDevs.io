PitchIT

Work is everywhere. Find it here.

A marketplace that connects people who need work done with the people who do it, across 13 sectors, from farming and fishing to trades, errands and professional services. It is built for how work really happens in Nigeria: directions from landmarks instead of street addresses, and jobs funded before the work begins.

Customer journey

  1. Post a job with landmarks and photos
  2. Compare offers and hire
  3. Fund the job and follow its progress
  4. Confirm the work and leave a review

Worker journey

  1. Discover jobs nearby
  2. Send an offer
  3. Chat and get directions
  4. Finish, get paid and build a reputation
  • Flutter
  • Supabase
  • Paystack
  • Realtime chat

From fixing systems to building products

“I build systems that leave echoes.”

I'm David Somadina Obichukwu, known online as xTc. I build products from end to end: I find the idea, design how it should feel, and build it until it works.

My route into building started on the practical side of technology. Installing systems, deploying products and supporting customers at Smile Tech taught me to start from the person using the product, and to make things that keep working when nobody is watching. Today I also evaluate AI output as an AI Data Annotation Specialist at Elevate Labs.

I work across software, product design and games. I can read, write and debug code on my own, and I use AI coding assistants as tools, not as a replacement for understanding what ships. Toilet Wars, PitchIT, EchoMind and the client sites are very different products, but they come from the same habit: take an idea seriously enough to finish it.

Approach

Start from a real problem, design the experience, then build and test until it holds up. AI speeds up drafting and debugging; I review, change and test what it produces.

Interests

Marketplaces and the informal economy, calm and private software, games, creative tools and the systems behind them.

Direction
