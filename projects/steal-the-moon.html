Steal the Moon

Build up. Then take the moon.

In development

Steal the Moon is a multiplayer tycoon and heist game in development on Roblox. Players claim a base, collect resources, upgrade, defend what they have and rob other players, on the way to the biggest heist imaginable: the moon itself.

Ask me about this project
Type
Multiplayer tycoon and heist game on Roblox
Built with
Roblox Studio, Luau
My role
Concept, game design and development

The idea

Tycoon games are about growth: start small, build up, unlock more. Steal the Moon gives that growth a goal worth chasing and adds risk: what you collect can be stolen, so you have to defend as well as build.

The gameplay loop

  1. Claim a base

    Each player claims a base of their own when they join.

  2. Collect

    Gather resources around the world and from your base to power the economy.

  3. Upgrade

    Spend what you collect on upgrades that make your base produce and protect more.

  4. Defend and rob

    Protect your base from other players, or travel to theirs and take what you can.

  5. Come back

    Daily rewards and long term goals bring players back to repeat the loop.

Systems being built

  • Claimable player bases and base ownership
  • Resource collection and a farm
  • Robbery and defence mechanics
  • Transport and portals between areas
  • A HUD, inventory and shops
  • A daily spin, a rarity mixer, the Moonlog and upgrades
  • Progression that rewards repeat play

Monetisation principles

Robux products have to be things players genuinely want. No pay to win, no fake scarcity and no pressure tactics, and existing player data and balances are always preserved through updates.
