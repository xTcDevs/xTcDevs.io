FinTech and Banking Interfaces

Interfaces that earn trust with money.

UI/UX exploration

A series of interface explorations for financial products: banking dashboards, transaction layouts, account management, authentication and everyday workflows, built with AI assistance and designed for Nigerian users first. These are design and front end studies. I have not built a bank, a payment processor or production banking infrastructure.

Why it matters

Money interfaces have to earn trust quickly. These studies are where I practise clear hierarchy, careful states, form validation and honest numbers.

What I explored

  • Banking dashboards and financial data presentation
  • Transaction layouts and histories
  • Account management
  • Authentication and user management interfaces
  • Form validation
  • Financial product UI/UX

The studies

  • Vault+ balance platformBuilt

    A deposit notification interface with separate user and admin panels, Naira formatting and simulated SMS alerts.

  • Bilingual banking appBuilt

    A Korean and English banking app mockup with sign in, a dashboard, alerts and a profile picture picker.
