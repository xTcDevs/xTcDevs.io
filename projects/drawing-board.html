AI Drawing Board

Think on a canvas, not in a chat.

Concept

A product concept for a spatial thinking tool for founders. Instead of a long chat thread, ideas live on a canvas where they can be arranged, connected and worked through with AI.

The idea

Founders think in maps: markets, users, features and risks that all connect. Chat tools force that into a single line of messages. A canvas lets the structure of an idea show.

Directions being weighed

  • A sharp first version for indie founders
  • A canvas built on tldraw
  • Freemium pricing
  • Launching to communities such as Product Hunt and Indie Hackers

Open questions

  • Which single founder task the first version should nail
  • How AI shows up on the canvas without cluttering it
