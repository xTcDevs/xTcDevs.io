xTc Personal AI Studio

Footage in, a considered edit out.

Prototype

A personal AI powered creative studio that brings video editing, photo editing and audio work into one place. I give it footage and a creative direction, and it makes editing decisions with an understanding of what happens in the clip. Today an experimental Python and FFmpeg engine exists; the studio around it is still the plan.

The problem

I want edits with taste and intention, from gaming montages and football highlights to cinematic, vlog, travel and promo cuts, without cutting every frame by hand and without depending on expensive AI subscriptions.

Existing prototype and product vision

  • Video processing engineBuilt

    A Python and FFmpeg pipeline that cuts, sequences and renders clips automatically.

  • Event understandingPlanned

    Cuts that follow what actually happens, such as a kill or a goal, instead of random cuts.

  • Edit stylesPlanned

    Gaming montages, cinematic, vlog, promo, travel and football highlight edits.

  • Effects, transitions and music syncPlanned

    Transitions and effects only where they help, with cuts placed on the beat of the music.

  • Voice and audioPlanned

    Audio placement, voiceover integration, and original audio kept by default.

  • Photo editingPlanned

    Photo edits in the same studio and the same visual styles.

  • Desktop interfacePlanned

    A visual studio on top of the engine, deliberately left until the engine is right.

Built means it runs today in the experimental engine. Everything else is the direction of the product.

Ground rules

  • Local first: it has to run on an everyday laptop
  • Outside AI only where it truly helps
  • Original audio stays unless I say otherwise
  • Built for my own creative work first

What testing taught me

The first real test, a 30 second gaming clip with two kills, produced random cuts, effects that were not needed and missing original audio. That result set the direction: the studio has to understand events before it decides where to cut, and the original sound stays by default.
